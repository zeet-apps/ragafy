Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Recall app (developed by Zeet Apps) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Recall lets you save links, notes, and tags for later. The content you save — URLs, titles, notes, and tags — is:

  • Entered or shared by you (typed directly, or shared into the app from another app such as a browser or social media app)
  • Stored locally on your device in the app's own database
  • Not sent to our servers or any third party as part of normal use

Recall does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use its core save/organize features.

Third-Party Services

Recall uses a small number of third-party services to run ads, process purchases, and catch crashes. Each only receives the minimum data it needs to do its job — none of them receive the content you save (your links, notes, or tags).

Service Purpose Data involved
Google AdMob Shows ads to free-tier users Advertising identifiers and standard mobile ad request data, per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK users), ad personalization consent is requested before personalized ads are shown.
RevenueCat Manages the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and processes subscription entitlements Purchase/entitlement status and an anonymous app-generated user ID. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are handled entirely by Apple/Google and never reach Recall or RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
Sentry Crash and error reporting, so we can fix bugs Crash logs, stack traces, and basic device/OS information. Does not include the content you've saved. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.
Apple App Store / Google Play Payment processing for in-app purchases Handled entirely by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies. Recall never sees your payment information.

Permissions

Recall requests internet access (to serve ads, validate purchases, and report crashes) and, on iOS, the ability to receive content shared from other apps via the system Share Sheet. Recall does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share the links, notes, or tags you save with anyone. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, and only to the extent needed for them to provide their function (serving ads, processing purchases, reporting crashes).

Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved items are stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual items at any time within the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. Recall does not currently store your saved content on any server, so there is no separate account to delete.

Children's Privacy

Recall is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Since your saved content lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times — you can view, edit, or delete it within the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. Depending on your location, you may also have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA regarding data held by the third-party services listed above; see their respective privacy policies for how to exercise those rights.

Future Changes: Cloud Sync

We plan to introduce an optional Cloud Sync feature in a future update, allowing your saved items to sync across your devices via a secure backend. This feature will be opt-in, and this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is synced and how before that feature is released.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
