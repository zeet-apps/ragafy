Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Recall app (developed by Zeet Apps) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Recall lets you save links, notes, and tags for later. The content you save — URLs, titles, notes, and tags — is:

  • Entered or shared by you (typed directly, or shared into the app from another app such as a browser or social media app)
  • Stored locally on your device in the app's own database
  • Not sent to our servers or any third party as part of normal use

Recall does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use its core save/organize features.

Third-Party Services

This version of Recall does not show ads, does not offer purchases, and does not include any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service. Nothing about you or your use of the app is sent to Zeet Apps or to any third party.

The one network activity is link previews: when you save a link, Recall contacts that link's own website to fetch its page title and preview image, the same way a web browser would. That website can see the request, as it would for any visitor. Previews are cached on your device.

Permissions

Recall requests internet access (to fetch link titles and preview images) and, on iOS, the ability to receive content shared from other apps via the system Share Sheet. Recall does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not collect it, and we do not share the links, notes, or tags you save with anyone.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved items are stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual items at any time within the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. Recall does not currently store your saved content on any server, so there is no separate account to delete.

Children's Privacy

Recall is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Since your saved content lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times — you can view, edit, or delete it within the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. Because Recall does not collect personal data, there is no data held by us to access or erase.

Future Changes

Future versions may add ads, an optional one-time purchase to remove them, or optional sync between your devices. If so, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and by whom before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
