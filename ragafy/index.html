How to practise

You do not need to sing every exercise in every raga. Learn it well in Mayamalavagowla first, then try it in Shankarabharanam, Kalyani and Kharaharapriya. Between them these four ragas use all twelve notes, so the same pattern trains your ear and voice on every swara. These four are only a suggestion: practise in whichever ragas you like.

  • MayamalavagowlaS R1 G3 M1 P D1 N3
  • ShankarabharanamS R2 G3 M1 P D2 N3
  • KalyaniS R2 G3 M2 P D2 N3
  • KharaharapriyaS R2 G2 M1 P D2 N2

Ragafy is a practice companion, not a replacement for a teacher. The lessons are plain notes; gamakas are best learnt from your guru.
