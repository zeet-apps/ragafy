Privacy Policy – Brain Boost

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Brain Boost is a quiz app for everyone, from children aged 4 and up to adults. This policy explains what the app keeps and where. In short: everything stays on your device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information the app keeps

  • A nickname you choose, so the app can greet you. It does not need to be your real name.
  • An age group (for example 5–7, 8–11, 12–17 or 18 and over) that you pick, so the app can show suitable quizzes. We never ask for a date of birth.
  • Your progress: which quizzes and levels you have finished and your scores.

All of this is stored only on your device. You can change the nickname and age group in Settings, and you can remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app currently has no links to other websites and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for internet access.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information, there is nothing to share about a child. The nickname and age group a child enters never leave the device. Parents and guardians can clear them at any time in Settings or by clearing the app's data. If you have a question about children and this app, please contact us.

Security

Data is kept only in the app's private storage on your device, so it is protected by your device's own security. We cannot see it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
