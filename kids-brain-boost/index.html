Kids Brain Boost

Quiz-based learning practice for kids.

About

Kids Brain Boost is an Android app for children aged 5 and up. Short quizzes cover alphabets, countries, science, math, stories and more.

  • Quiz-based learning across many subject batches
  • Portrait layout for phones and tablets
  • Name and progress stay on the device; no analytics or advertising
