Music Note Trainer

Learn music staff notation through interactive lessons.

About

Music Note Trainer helps children aged 5 and up learn to read notes on the staff, level by level.

  • Level 1: natural notes (C D E F G A B)
  • Level 2: several notes together
  • Level 3: sharps and flats
  • Progress saved on the device; nothing is sent anywhere
